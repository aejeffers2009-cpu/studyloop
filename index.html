LOCAL PROFILES

Your study space is saved in this browser.

Important: these are local profiles, not online accounts. They do not use passwords, sync across devices, or create a private account for a friend. For friends to access a shared deck, send them a share link and make sure they can open the hosted StudyLoop page.

Share a flashcard set

The link includes the selected set's terms and definitions. Anyone with the link can view its contents, so don't share private or sensitive information.
